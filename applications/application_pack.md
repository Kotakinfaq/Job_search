# Application Pack: 48 Jobs

Everything you need to apply, in ranked order (UCSI excluded). For each job:
**where to apply → which CV to attach → the message to paste**.

## Before you start (one-time setup, ~1 hour)

1. **Export both CVs as PDF** from the `cv/` folder: `Prama_Putra_CV_Design_Video.pdf` and `Prama_Putra_CV_Data.pdf`.
2. **Get a portfolio folder ready** (Google Drive, "Anyone with the link can view"): 6–8 designs, 2–3 short videos, 6–8 photos. You chose to leave it off the CV, but many of these listings ask for samples, so have the link ready to paste.
3. **Create or update accounts:** JobStreet ID, JobStreet MY, Indeed (one account covers ID and MY), Glints, Dealls, Projects.co.id, Upwork, Remotive. Upload your CV once to each; after that most jobs are one-click apply.
4. **Personalize before sending.** Read each posting and change one sentence so it mentions something specific about the company. Remove the hook sentence if it no longer fits.
5. **Check the posting date.** Skip anything older than about 30 days or marked closed.
6. **Scam rule:** never pay a deposit, "registration fee" or "starter kit". Real jobs don't charge you.

## Order to apply

- **Week 1:** #1–#12, the highest chance of acceptance
- **Week 2:** #14–#33 (Indonesian, worldwide and AI-platform roles; the Malaysian ones are marked ⛔)
- **Week 3:** #34–#48, lower chance
- **⛔ Malaysian employers** (#13, #18–20, #23, #25, #29, #34, #40–42): wait until UCSI confirms you're allowed to work for them.

> ⚠️ **About 20–30 hours/week:** if you're on a Student Pass, Malaysian rules cap part-time work at **20 hours a week, during semester breaks only**. If a job asks about hours, 20 is the safe number to give until UCSI tells you otherwise.

After applying, update the **Status** and **Date applied** columns in `job_tracker.csv`. Follow up after 5–7 days if you hear nothing.
