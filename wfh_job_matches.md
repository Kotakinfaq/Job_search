## ⚠️ Read first: Student Pass rules (Malaysia)

If you're in Malaysia on a **Student Pass**, check this before taking paid work:

- Education Malaysia (EMGS) says international students may work part-time **max 20 hrs/week, only during semester breaks or holidays longer than 7 days**. It also needs **prior Immigration approval through your university**, and the listed sectors are restaurants, petrol kiosks, mini markets and hotels. ([EMGS FAQ](https://educationmalaysia.gov.my/Get-In-Touch/FAQ), [EMGS Hub](https://hub.emgs.com.my/?p=2436), [Univ. of Nottingham Malaysia](https://nottingham.edu.my/International/LifeinMalaysia/Working/index.aspx))
- Official guidance is not fully consistent ([Global Scholarships](https://globalscholarships.com/work-study-programs-malaysia/)). Remote freelancing for clients **outside** Malaysia (e.g. Indonesian or global clients) isn't clearly covered.
- **Action:** email **UCSI's International Student Office** and ask: *"Am I allowed to do remote freelance work for clients outside Malaysia while on my Student Pass?"* Get the answer in writing. A Student Pass violation can cost you your studies, and that risk is bigger than any side income.
- Safe options in the meantime: building your portfolio, unpaid student or campus projects, and internships that are part of your degree.

## 1. Social media content designer / admin (Indonesia-focused, remote) — **apply first**

**Why you'll get it:** You've already done this job (Lokality: +40% engagement, +30% followers). You can design, edit video, and shoot photos, which usually takes three different freelancers. Small Indonesian brands like hiring one person who can do all of it.

**Where to look**
- JobStreet Indonesia: [admin sosial media](https://id.jobstreet.com/id/admin-sosial-media-jobs) (~800+ listings), [freelance remote](https://id.jobstreet.com/id/freelance-remote-jobs). One example seen: *Social Media & Content Creator (Freelance), North Jakarta, part-time.*
- Indeed Indonesia: [admin sosial media, part time](https://id.indeed.com/q-admin-sosial-media,-part-time-lowongan.html), [social media remote](https://id.indeed.com/q-social-media-remote-lowongan.html)
- Glints, Kalibrr, Dealls, and LinkedIn with filters **Remote + Part-time/Freelance**. Search: `admin sosial media remote`, `content creator freelance`, `social media specialist part time`.
- Instagram/X hashtags: `#lokerremote`, `#lokerwfh`, `#lokeradminsosmed`
- **Direct outreach (highest hit rate):** pick 20 small F&B, skincare, or modest-fashion brands on Instagram with weak feeds. DM or email them a **free redesign of 3 of their posts** plus your rate card.

**Search keywords:** `social media admin remote`, `content designer part time`, `admin sosmed WFH`, `social media intern remote`

## 2. Short-form video editor (freelance)

**Why:** Demand for TikTok/Reels/Shorts editing is huge, and many listings only ask for **CapCut** ([Indeed ID remote video editor, 900+ results](https://id.indeed.com/q-remote-job,-video-editor-lowongan.html), e.g. TEG Studio, freelance TikTok/Reels editor, CapCut/Premiere). DaVinci gives you an edge for color grading.

**Leads found**
- [JobStreet ID: video editor remote](https://id.jobstreet.com/id/video-editor-remote-jobs) (~500 listings, incl. a Bali studio open to remote)
- [Remotive: remote video editing](https://remotive.com/remote-video-editing-jobs): part-time worldwide roles seen, incl. a *Video Editing Intern (Totem Global, part-time, worldwide)*
- [DailyRemote: video editor](https://dailyremote.com/remote-video-editor-jobs): a part-time role open to the Philippines, Singapore, and Thailand at $6–12/h, plus worldwide listings

**What to prepare:** a 30–60 s showreel plus 3 sample edits (talking-head with captions, product ad, travel/vlog). Many clients send a **paid or unpaid test edit**. Do it fast and well; that's how you win.

## 3. AI training / data annotation (Indonesian · English · Arabic)

**Why:** Selection is test-based, not CV-based, so your experience level matters less. Being trilingual and studying Data Science help. Hours are flexible, which suits student schedules.

**Leads found**
- **TELUS Digital AI Community** (formerly TELUS International AI): *AI Community Surfer – English, Indonesia*, part-time freelance; you compare search/AI responses ([LinkedIn listing](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/3517685940), [opportunities page](https://www.telusinternational.ai/opportunities)). Note: workers say Indonesian-locale address rating is slow ([review summary](https://earnifyhub.com/surveys-tasks/telus-international-ai-review-2026)).
- **OpenTrain AI**: [Indonesian-speaker AI training roles](https://www.opentrain.ai/jobs/language/id/) (~10 open)
- **Sigma Group**: [Indonesian Linguistic Projects (Remote, freelance)](https://careers.sigma.ai/jobs/5742302-indonesian-linguistic-projects-remote)
- **Foundit Indonesia**: [data annotation jobs](https://www.foundit.id/search/data-annotation-jobs) (Innodata, RWS, etc.)
- Also try: **Appen**, **Outlier**, **DataAnnotation**, **Toloka**. Search their sites for "Indonesian" and "Arabic". Availability by country changes often.
- **Skip** listings marked *US-only* (e.g. the Careerflow and Experis Indonesian annotator roles are US-resident only).

**Tip:** Apply to 4–5 platforms at once. Projects come and go, so having several accounts keeps work steady. Read the guidelines carefully before the qualification test; most people fail by rushing.

## 4. Stock contributor (passive income, builds your portfolio)

- **Adobe Stock Contributor / Shutterstock / Freepik Contributor:** upload photos, vectors (Illustrator), and PSD/social templates. Acceptance is per-file, not per-person, so you can start this week.
- **Canva Creators:** you're paid royalties when people use your templates. The template program has been in beta, the review can take months, and a portfolio of about 10 varied Canva designs is recommended ([Canva help](https://www.canva.com/help/canva-creators-program/), [overview](https://www.krea.ai/blog/can-you-make-money-with-canva-creators-program-and-etsy-templates-in-2026)). Apply now so the wait runs in the background.
- Income starts small, but every upload also becomes portfolio material.

## 5. Freelance graphic design on marketplaces

- **Indonesian:** [Projects.co.id](https://projects.co.id/public/browse_projects/listing/18_internet-marketing-and-social-media) (many small one-off jobs; some budgets are low, ~Rp 200–250k), Sribulancer, Fastwork.id
- **Global:** Fiverr, Upwork
- **Niche gigs with less competition:** *"Canva social media template pack"*, *"Indonesian/Arabic + English bilingual social posts"*, *"YouTube thumbnail + short edit bundle"*, *"product photo retouch & background removal"*. Your languages are a real differentiator here.
- Start with low prices for your first 3–5 reviews, then raise them.
