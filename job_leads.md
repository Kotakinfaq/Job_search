## How each lead is scored (out of 10)

| Factor | What raises the score |
|---|---|
| **Skill match** | Asks for what you have: Canva, Photoshop, CapCut, social media, Indonesian/English |
| **Entry barrier** | No years-of-experience requirement; a portfolio or test is enough |
| **Eligibility** | Open to someone living in Kuala Lumpur and not restricted to US, Indonesia or Malaysia residents |
| **Visa risk** | 🟢 no clear issue · 🟡 grey area, ask UCSI first · 🔴 Malaysian employer, needs Student Pass approval |

**Visa reminder:** On a Malaysian Student Pass, work for **Malaysian employers** needs Immigration approval through UCSI. It is limited to semester breaks, 20 h/week, and specific sectors. That's why Malaysian jobs are marked 🔴 even when the skill fit is good. Remote work for **Indonesian or overseas** clients isn't clearly covered, so it's marked 🟡. Ask UCSI's International Office in writing before you start paid work.

1. **Mon:** Apply to 3–5 roles from the Top 10 and sections A–C (tailored CV + portfolio link + the short pitch from `wfh_job_matches.md`).
